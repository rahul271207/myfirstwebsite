World War II was a global conflict that lasted from 1939 to 1945 and involved many countries around the world. The war began in Europe when Nazi Germany invaded Poland on September 1, 1939. The major opposing groups were the Axis Powers, including Germany, Italy, and Japan, and the Allied Powers, including Britain, France, the Soviet Union, China, and later the United States. Major events included the Battle of Stalingrad, the attack on Pearl Harbor, the D-Day landings, and the Holocaust. The war ended in Europe in May 1945 after Germany surrendered, while Japan surrendered in August 1945 after the atomic bombings of Hiroshima and Nagasaki. World War II caused enormous loss of life and destruction and significantly changed the political and economic order of the world.
